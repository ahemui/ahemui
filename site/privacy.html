Privacy

AhemUI doesn't collect anything. Captures stay on your computer and in your own git repository.

Last updated 29 September 2026

What AhemUI handles

When you capture feedback, AhemUI records a screenshot of the area you highlighted, the text you type, and details about the page: its address, the element and source file, styles, recent console errors and failed requests, and the clicks that led there.

Where it goes

  • To ahemui serve, a small server running on your own computer (127.0.0.1), which writes it into the .ahemui/ folder of your project. From there it's shared only if you commit and push it.
  • To Claude Code on your computer, when you or a Claude session read the backlog.
  • To your issue tracker (Linear, Jira, Notion, GitHub), only if you ask Claude to sync, through the connection you set up yourself.

AhemUI has no servers of its own, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. Nothing is sold or shared.

The browser extension

  • It runs on local development sites, and on other sites only after you switch it on for that site.
  • It takes a screenshot of the visible tab only when you capture feedback.
  • On deployed sites, password fields, anything marked private, email addresses, long numbers and URL query strings are masked or removed before anything leaves the page.
  • It stores your per-site project choices and settings in the browser's extension storage.

Settings

Your personal settings (toolbar corner, notifications and so on) are saved in ~/.ahemui/prefs.json on your computer.
